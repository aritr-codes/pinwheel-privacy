Pinwheel Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Pinwheel is a personal Windows desktop application used by its developer to prepare and publish Pins to the developer's own Pinterest business account. It is not a hosted service and has no other users.

What Pinwheel accesses

  • Image files and metadata (titles, descriptions, links, board names) that the user selects on their own computer.
  • With the user's permission, granted through Pinterest's official OAuth sign-in, the user's own Pinterest boards and the ability to create Pins on them.

What Pinwheel does not do

  • It never asks for, stores or sees Pinterest passwords or browser session cookies.
  • It does not collect data about other Pinterest users.
  • It has no servers, analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party data sharing.
  • It does not sell, rent or transfer any data.

Where data is stored

Everything stays on the user's own computer: the working queue, a local record of published Pins, and the Pinterest access token (kept in the Windows Credential Manager). Pin content is sent only to Pinterest, through the Pinterest API, when the user chooses to publish a Pin.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings under connected apps. Deleting Pinwheel's local files and its Credential Manager entry removes all data it stored.
